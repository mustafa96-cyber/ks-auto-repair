Accessibility Statement

Effective date: September 15, 2026

K & S Auto Repair wants every visitor to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities. We believe getting your car serviced should be easy for everyone, online and in person.

Our commitment

We are working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.1, level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content easier to use for people with a wide range of abilities. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we keep improving the site over time.

What we do

  • We use clear text, headings, and labels so the page is easy to read and follow.
  • The site works with a keyboard, and buttons and links have descriptive text.
  • We aim for readable color contrast and text that scales on phones and tablets.
  • Images that carry meaning include text descriptions.

Things to know

The moving three dimensional graphics on the home page are decorative. You do not need them to understand the page or to request service, and every action is also available as plain text and buttons. The map on our contact section is provided by Google, which manages its own accessibility.

Need help, or found a problem?

If any part of this website is hard to use, or you would rather set up your service by phone, please reach out. We are glad to help you directly, and your feedback helps us fix issues quickly.
