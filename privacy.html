Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

K & S Auto Repair (we, us, our) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us:

  • When you send a service request through the form, we collect your name, phone number, the service you selected, and any details you add.
  • When you call, text, or email us, we receive whatever information you share in that message.

We do not ask for payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or account passwords on this website, and we do not require you to create an account.

How we use your information

We use your information only to respond to you, to schedule and perform the service you asked about, and to contact you about your vehicle or request. We do not use it for anything unrelated.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Service requests submitted through the form are delivered to our email inbox using a third party form delivery service that passes the message to us. We may share information only when it is needed to provide a service you asked for, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not build a profile of your browsing. Pages such as our map may be provided by a third party (Google Maps) that has its own privacy practices.

Your choices and California privacy rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and we will honor that request. Because we do not sell personal information, there is nothing to opt out of. To make a request, call or email us using the details below.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your request. Standard message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Children

This website is meant for adults seeking auto service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
